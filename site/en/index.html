About me

Holistic beauty, made with heart

I'm Maria, and I've worked as a holistic beauty therapist for over 10 years. I see and treat the body as a whole, combining techniques such as Ayurveda and Lomi Lomi. I originally come from Ecuador and speak fluent German, Spanish and English. I bring warmth to my work and create exactly the right balance of relaxation and vitality for every client.
